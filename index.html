    Rules of the Variant

    Whenever you draw a Mythos card during the Mythos Phase, look at its Clue Appears At: entry. For each Clue that Mythos card would place on the board — whether or not there is a gate at those locations — instead draw one Alternate Clues card and follow the instructions on it.

    If an Alternate Clues card would place something in a location with a gate, it does nothing. A Mythos card that places no Clues doesn't warrant a card being drawn, and one that places several Clue tokens warrants that many cards drawn. A gate opening in a location that contains Alternate Clues objects discards all those objects just as it would discard Clue tokens.

    Alternate Clues cards don't just add Clue tokens: they add items, money, special conditions and more. Random Common and Unique Items, Spells, Skills and Allies are placed at the location face down, and are turned face up only when picked up. For the most part they follow the base game's rules for picking up Clue tokens: you pick up any and all objects at a location if and only if you end your movement there, and you must pick up everything you can.

    Specific cases

    Sanity and Stamina
    Place the tokens in the location. An investigator picking them up gains that much Sanity or Stamina. You only pick up enough to heal to full, and leave the rest behind.
    Special cards (Bank Loan, Rail Pass, etc.)
    An investigator can only pick up a special card — one there are only eight copies of — if they don't already have one. It stays in the location, waiting for someone else. If the supply has run out, the card does nothing and you don't draw a replacement.
    Specific cards
    Search the correct deck for the named card and place it at the location. If there are none left in the deck, ignore the Alternate Clues card and don't draw a replacement.
    Gate and monster trophies
    Place these trophies in the location upside down, to tell them apart from real gates and monsters.
    Inner Sanctum
    You pick up items at the Inner Sanctum by ending your movement in the Silver Twilight Lodge while holding a Silver Twilight Membership. Keep the Lodge's and the Sanctum's objects clearly separate. A gate opening at the Lodge discards the Sanctum's items too.
    Streets, Other Worlds, Lost in Time and Space, Jail Cells
    The same rules apply: you pick up objects by ending your movement there. In an Other World you only end your movement in the second area if you stay — someone who ports out with Find Gate doesn't gain the Clue token.
    Bank Loan
    An investigator who has previously defaulted on a Bank Loan can't pick one up.
    Blessing
    Picking up a Blessing works exactly like being Blessed: it either Blesses you or removes a current Curse.
    Captain of the White Ship
    Follow the card. If someone is already the Captain, ignore it and don't draw a replacement. You must remove the card from the location even if you don't use the White Ship ability that turn — the offer only stands once.
    Changed
    Follow the card. If someone is already Changed, ignore it and don't draw a replacement.
    Charter token
    Follow the card. A spare aquatic location marker, or any other makeshift solution, works if you'd rather not use the token.
    Dunwich Horror token
    Follow the card. You can only place one if the Dunwich Horror isn't on the board, and if a game effect needs a token while the only one left is at a location, it is taken from there — there's no slowing Wilbur's brother.
    Innsmouth Look
    Follow the card. If you hold a "You don't have the Innsmouth Look" card, everyone else is slightly more at risk of that foul ancestry. If you bear the Look, maybe get out of Innsmouth.
    Rift Progress token
    Follow the card. If a game effect needs a token while the only one left is at a location, it is taken from there.
    Uprising Clue token
    Follow the card. A Clue token with a Sanity token on top, or any other makeshift solution, works instead.
