## Rules (from the post itself):
"The rules of the variant are simple: whenever you draw a Mythos card during the Mythos Phase, look at the Clue Appears At: -entry of that card. For each Clue that Mythos card would place on the board (whether or not there is a gate at those locations), instead draw one Alternate Clues card, and follow the instructions therein. If an Alternate Clues card would place something in a location with a gate, it does nothing. A Mythos card that places no Clues on the board doesn't warrant an Alternate Clue card being drawn, and one that places several Clue tokens warrants that many cards drawn. A gate opening in a location that contains Alternate Clues -placed objects discards all those objects just as it would discard Clue tokens.
Only, that's not where the fun stops. That's because Alternate Clues cards don't just add Clue tokens to the board: they can add a variety of things besides. Items, money, special conditions, you name it! Random Common and Unique Items, Spells, Skills and Allies are placed at the location face down, and are turned face up only when picked up. The specific rules for these cases are listed below, but for the most part they follow the rules for picking up Clue tokens from the base game: you pick up any and all objects at a location if and only if you end your movement in that location. An investigator has to pick up any and all objects they can when they end their movement (exceptions listed below)."

"This sections lists the specifics of certain Alternate Clues cards where confusion might otherwise arise. Just remember that in most cases you pick up things at locations when you end your movement in that location.

Sanity and Stamina: When an Alternate Clues card tells you to place Sanity and Stamina tokens into a location, you do just that. When an investigator picks up Sanity and Stamina tokens, they gain that much Sanity/Stamina, exactly as you'd expect. You only pick up enough Sanity and Stamina to heal you to full, and leave the rest at the location.

Special cards (Bank Loan, Rail Pass etc.): An investigator can only pick up a special card (that is, a card there's only eight copies of) if they don't have such a card already. An investigator with a Bank Loan who ends their movement in a location containing a Bank Loan, for example, won't pick it up, but the card also doesn't disappear - it stays in that location, waiting for another investigator.

Note that if the supply for a given special card has run out (for example, every player is Blessed), then an Alternate Cards stating a Blessing is placed at a location doesn't do anything. Don't draw another card to replace it.

Specific cards: When a card states that a specific card (for example a Whiskey Common Item) is to appear at a location, search for that card in the correct deck and place it at that location. If there are no such cards left in the deck (every Whiskey card is currently in play, for example), ignore the Alternate Clues card and don't draw another card to replace it.

Gate and monster trophies: Place these trophies in the location upside down, to differentiate them from gates and monsters at that location.

Inner Sanctum: As mentioned on the Alternate Clues cards, you pick up items at the Inner Sanctum by ending your movement in Silver Twilight Lodge while having a Silver Twilight Membership; separate the objects at the Lodge and the Sanctum clearly from each other. A gate opening at the Lodge discards all items at the Sanctum as well.

Clues at streets, in Other Worlds, in lost in time and space and in Jail Cells: The same rules apply here as they do on locations: you pick up objects when you end your movement in that location. In the case of Other Worlds, you only end your movement in the second area if you stay at the Other World: someone who ports out of there with Find Gate doesn't gain the Clue token.

Bank Loan: An investigator who has previously defaulted on a Bank Loan can't pick one up.

Blessing: Picking up a Blessing card works exactly like being Blessed: it either Blesses you or removes a current Curse.

Captain of the White Ship: Follow the rules on the card. If someone already is the Captain when the Alternate Clues card is drawn, ignore that card and don't draw another card to replace it. Note that you have to remove the card from the location even if you don't use the White Ship ability that turn: the offer only stands once.

Changed: Follow the rules on the card. If someone already is Changed when the Alternate Clues card is drawn, ignore that card and don't draw another card to replace it.

Charter token: Follow the rules on the card. If you don't want to use the token provided, an excess aquatic location marker, or any other such makeshift solution, is fine.

Dunwich Horror token: Follow the rules on the card. Note that you can only place such a token if the Dunwich Horror isn't currently on the board, and if a game effect requires a Dunwich Horror token to be placed on the track and the only one left is at a location, it's taken from that location: there's no slowing Wilbur's brother.

Innsmouth Look: Follow the rules on the card. Note that if you have a "You don't have the Innsmouth Look" -card, then other people are (slightly) more at risk of bearing that foul ancestry - plan accordingly. Likewise, if you bear the "You have the Innsmouth Look" card, maybe get out of Innsmouth.

Rift Progress token: Follow the rules on the card. Note that if a game effect requires a Rift Progress token to be placed on the track and the only one left is at a location, it's taken from that location.

Uprising Clue token: Follow the rules on the card. If you don't want to use the token provided, a Clue token with a Sanity token on top, or any other such makeshift solution, is fine."
